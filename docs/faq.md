## 🔹 What does “67” actually mean?

“**67**” (also written 6-7, “six seven”) is a viral internet meme and cultural signal that gained traction in early 2025, particularly among Gen Z and Gen Alpha.

According to *Know Your Meme*, the trend traces back to the song  
“Doot Doot” — Skrilla ft. G Herbo (Dec 2024).

In January 2025:
- “6-7” became a bait-and-switch phrase in TikTok & Instagram Reels  
- Often used in LaMelo Ball highlight edits (he is 6'7")  

The meme evolved into:
- Forced “six seven” call-outs on camera  
- A recognizable hand gesture (see-saw motion, palms up)  
- Spoken phrases like *“six, seven”*  

Mainstream acceleration occurred after a viral Cam Wilder basketball video where a kid yelled “Ay, 6-7.”  
That kid was later identified as Maverick Trevillian, cementing “67” as a cultural reference.
